# The Relic of Marethion

[THe search of the ancient relic.pdf](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/24637979/THe.search.of.the.ancient.relic.pdf)

Long ago, the underwater kingdom **Aquantis** was protected by a
powerful artifact known as the **Relic of Marethion**.\
The relic kept the seas calm and filled the ocean with life.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Shattered Relic

But during a great undersea quake, the relic shattered into two pieces
and was lost inside dangerous caves.\
Without it, Aquantis collapsed, and the ocean slowly fell into chaos.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Your Mission

You are a deep-sea diver following your grandfather's final journal.\
He believed the relic was real... and that someone brave enough could
restore it.

Armed only with your suit and your courage, you descend into the blue
to:

-   **Explore the caves of Aquantis**
-   **Dodge ancient guardians**
-   **Recover both relic fragments**
-   **Restore balance to the sea**

But beware --- the creatures defending the relic will *reset your
journey* if they touch you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Become the Savior of Aquantis

Return the relic to the surface...\
and become the one who saves the forgotten kingdom beneath the waves.
